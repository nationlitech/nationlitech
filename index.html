About us

An ancient and elegant Chinese saying goes: “In heaven there is paradise,on earth there are Suzhou and Hangzhou." Our company is situated in Hangzhou, one of these "paradises" and a celebrated scenic destination.

Famous for West Lake Longjing tea — a signature of Chinese green tea — Hangzhou has fostered a complete industrial chain for tea production equipment. As the central hub of Zhejiang Province, a region globally recognized for its robust manufacturing sector, Hangzhou offers unique industrial advantages that we fully leverage.

Boasting decades of trade experience, our company has established stable cooperative relationships with hundreds of production equipment manufacturers in Zhejiang and across China. We not only provide one-stop comprehensive tea production equipment but also offer tailor-made solutions to meet specific customer needs.

Professionalism, sincerity, prudence and efficiency are our core values, and we are committed to upholding these principles in every cooperation. We anticipate the opportunity to support your business in achieving greater financial growth.

Our Products

  • Tea Plucking Machine

    High-efficiency tea plucking equipment, suitable for large-scale tea garden harvesting.

  • Fresh Tea Leaf Conveyor

    Gentle and stable transportation equipment for fresh tea leaves, preserving leaf integrity during processing.

  • Tea Breaker Machine

    Efficiently crushes tea leaves to ideal particle size, enhancing the extraction of tea flavors.

  • Tea Cooling Machine

    Quickly cools processed tea leaves to lock in freshness and prevent over-fermentation.

  • Tea Drying Machine

    Uniformly removes excess moisture from tea leaves, ensuring long-term storage stability.

  • Tea Fermentation Machine

    Precisely controls temperature and humidity to achieve optimal tea fermentation effects.

  • Tea Rolling Machine

    Kneads tea leaves to release internal juices, enhancing the rich flavor and aroma of tea.

  • Tea Sorting Machine

    Classifies tea leaves by size and quality, ensuring consistent standards for finished products.

  • Tea Weighing & Packaging Machine

    Automatically weighs and packs tea products, improving packaging efficiency and accuracy.

  • Withering Machine

    Reduces tea leaf moisture content naturally, laying a foundation for subsequent processing steps.
